About Flow AI

Flow AI is an academic IoT project focused on real-time people flow monitoring through embedded systems, sensor fusion, and AI. The goal is to create smarter environments that understand and adapt to human movement.

Project Objectives

  • Develop a dual-architecture IoT system integrating STM32 and Raspberry Pi.
  • Design reliable communication protocols for sensor synchronization.
  • Implement AI-based flow analysis and a responsive chatbot interface.

The Team

Flow AI was developed by IoT engineering students from ECE Lyon (2025). The project embodies interdisciplinary collaboration across hardware, software, and AI domains.
